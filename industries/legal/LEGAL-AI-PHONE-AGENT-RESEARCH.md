4. Intake is structured: charges, arrest date, bail status, court date

## 6. ETHICAL & COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

### ABA Formal Opinion 512 (July 2024) — First AI Ethics Guidance

1. **Competence (Rule 1.1)**: Lawyers must understand AI capabilities/limitations
2. **Confidentiality (Rule 1.6)**: Client data input to AI must be secure
3. **Communication (Rule 1.4)**: Should disclose AI use to clients
4. **Supervision (Rules 5.1, 5.3)**: Must establish policies and training for AI
5. **Fees (Rule 1.5)**: Cannot charge hourly for time "saved" by AI

### What an AI Phone Agent CAN Do (Safe)

- Collect factual information (names, dates, incident details)
- Schedule consultations
- Share general firm info (practice areas, hours, locations, general fee structures)
- Route calls to appropriate attorneys
- Collect names for conflict screening (human review)
- Send follow-up confirmations
- Flag urgency without interpreting law

### What an AI Phone Agent CANNOT Do (UPL Risk)

- Assess merits or strength of a case
- Advise on legal strategy
- Interpret statutes or regulations
- Recommend whether to accept a settlement
- Make determinations about which claims to pursue
- Provide case-specific legal analysis

### Required Disclaimers

1. Disclose AI status at start of every call
2. "This is not legal advice — I'm here to gather information for the attorney"
3. All call data encrypted and accessible only to the firm
4. Attorney must supervise AI system's prompts and responses

### Client Comfort (Clio 2024)

- **51%** of prospective clients found chatbots helpful as starting point
- **50%+** comfortable using chatbots for simple legal questions
- **60%** would use chatbots only if they could switch to human

## 8. RECOMMENDED AGENT BUILD

### Agent Type: After-Hours Overflow + Legal Intake

### Primary Target: Personal Injury Firms (solo/small, 1-10 attorneys)

### Core Capabilities:
1. **24/7 answering** with instant pickup (no hold time)
2. **Practice area routing** — identify PI, criminal, family, immigration
3. **Structured intake** — collect all required case info per practice area
4. **Urgency flagging** — criminal arrests, DV situations, SOL concerns
5. **Warm transfer** — hot-transfer urgent calls to attorney's cell
6. **Conflict screening** — collect opposing party names for human review
7. **Bilingual** — English and Spanish
8. **Post-call summary** — text/email the attorney with structured intake data
9. **Appointment scheduling** — book consultations for next business day
10. **Ethical compliance** — AI disclosure, no legal advice, confidentiality

### Voice & Personality:
- Professional, warm, empathetic (people calling lawyers are stressed/scared)
- NOT robotic — natural speech patterns, verbal acknowledgments
- Calm and reassuring — especially for criminal/DV/injury callers
- Confident but never authoritative about legal matters
