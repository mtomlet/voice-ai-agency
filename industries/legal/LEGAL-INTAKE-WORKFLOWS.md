# Legal Intake Voice Agent - Detailed Workflows

> Compiled Feb 11, 2026. Sources: NACDL, ILRC, CasePeer, EvenUp, FindLaw, Answering Legal, ABA Formal Opinion 512, Clio, Smith.ai, 25+ sources.

---

## 1. PERSONAL INJURY INTAKE (Highest Value)

### Call Flow (15-25 minutes)

**Phase 1: Warm Opening (2-3 min)**
- Greet warmly, introduce yourself/the firm
- Express empathy: "I'm sorry to hear about what happened"
- Set expectations: "I'd like to ask you a few questions to understand your situation so we can see how we might be able to help"
- Ask permission: "Would it be alright if I ask you a few specific questions?"

**Phase 2: Core Screening Questions (8-12 min)**

| Category | Specific Questions |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Incident Type** | What type of accident? (auto, slip/fall, medical malpractice, workplace, product liability, dog bite, etc.) |
| **Date of Incident** | When did the accident happen? (CRITICAL for statute of limitations) |
| **Location** | Where did the accident occur? (state, city, public/private property — determines jurisdiction and SOL) |
| **What Happened** | In your own words, can you tell me what happened? |
| **Fault/Liability** | Who do you believe was at fault? Was a police report filed? Were any citations issued? Were there witnesses? |
| **Injuries Sustained** | What injuries did you suffer? Where do you feel pain? Did you lose consciousness? |
| **Medical Treatment** | Did you go to the hospital/ER? Have you seen a doctor since? Are you currently receiving treatment? What treatments have you had? (surgery, physical therapy, etc.) |
| **Current Status** | How are you doing now? Are you back to work? Do you have any physical limitations now? |
| **Insurance** | Do you have health insurance? What is the at-fault party's insurance? Have you been contacted by any insurance company? Did you give a recorded statement? |
| **Attorney** | Have you spoken with or hired any other attorney about this? |
| **Property Damage** | Was there property damage? (vehicle, personal items) |
| **Referral Source** | How did you hear about us? |

**Phase 3: Contact Information (2-3 min)**
- Full legal name, date of birth
- Phone number (text-capable preferred), email
- Best time and method to reach them
- Mailing address

**Phase 4: Wrap-Up (2-3 min)**
- Explain next steps clearly
- Set callback expectation (within 24 hours is standard)
- Provide direct contact info for follow-up questions
- Thank them and reassure

### What the Firm Needs to Determine

1. **Is there liability?** (someone else was at fault)
2. **Are there damages?** (real injuries requiring medical treatment, with ongoing impact)
3. **Is it timely?** (statute of limitations has not expired)

### Disqualifying Factors (Immediate Rejection)

1. **Statute of Limitations Expired** — automatic disqualification (most states: 2-3 years)
2. **No Permanent/Significant Injury** — client fully recovered, no ongoing treatment
3. **Outside Practice Area** — divorce, bankruptcy, criminal. Should refer, not reject
4. **Client Was Clearly At Fault** — "I ran the red light"
5. **No Treatment / Large Gap** — never saw a doctor, or waited 6+ months
6. **Already Represented** — already has attorney (conflict/ethics issue)
7. **Workers' Comp Only** — workplace injury with no third-party liability

## 2. CRIMINAL DEFENSE INTAKE

### Call Flow

**Phase 1: Immediate Situation Assessment**
- Are you currently in custody / jail?
- Have you been arrested? When?
- Have you been charged? What are the charges?
- Do you have a court date? When? (CRITICAL — may be within 24-72 hours)
- Are you out on bail/bond? What are the conditions?
- Do you have a public defender or another attorney?

**Phase 2: Case Details**
- What happened? (brief version)
- When and where did the incident occur?
- Who was the alleged victim? Relationship?
- Were there witnesses?
- Were you read your rights (Miranda)?
- Did you make any statements to police?
- Was there a search? Did you consent?
- Was alcohol or drugs involved?

**Phase 3: Charge-Specific Questions**

| Charge Type | Additional Questions |
|-------------|---------------------|
| **DUI/DWI** | BAC level? Field sobriety tests? Breathalyzer/blood? Refusal? Prior DUIs? DMV hearing deadline (10-15 days) |
| **Domestic Violence** | Relationship to alleged victim? Protective order? Children? Injuries alleged? |
| **Drug Charges** | What substance? Amount? Possession vs. distribution? Drug-free zone? |
| **Assault/Battery** | Weapon involved? Injuries? Self-defense? |
| **Theft/Fraud** | Amount involved? (misdemeanor vs. felony threshold) |

### Urgency Factor: EXTREME

| Scenario | Urgency |
|----------|---------|
| Currently in jail | IMMEDIATE |
| Just arrested, released | WITHIN 24 HOURS |
| DUI arrest | WITHIN 10-15 DAYS (DMV hearing) |
| Domestic violence | IMMEDIATE |
| Warrant outstanding | HIGH |

## 3. IMMIGRATION INTAKE

### Call Flow

**Phase 1: Biographical Information**
- Full legal name (as on documents)
- Date of birth, country of birth, nationality
- Current U.S. address
- Phone, email, preferred language
- Marital status, spouse's citizenship
- Children (ages, citizenship)

**Phase 2: Immigration Status**
- Current immigration status? (citizen, LPR, visa holder, undocumented, TPS, DACA, asylum)
- When does status/visa expire?
- When and how did you last enter the U.S.?
- Valid passport?
- Prior removal/deportation proceedings?
- Prior visa/green card denials?

**Phase 3: Case-Type Specific**

| Case Type | Key Questions |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Family Petition** | U.S. citizen/LPR family member who could petition? |
| **Employment/H-1B** | Job offer? Employer sponsorship? Education? |
| **Asylum** | Why afraid to return? What happened? When arrived? (1-year deadline) |
| **Deportation Defense** | Notice to Appear? Next court date? In detention? |
| **DACA** | When arrived? Age at arrival? Prior DACA? Expiration? |
| **Crime Victims** | U-visa/T-visa/VAWA eligibility? |

### Urgency by Case Type

| Level | Case Type |
|-------|-----------|
| EMERGENCY | Deportation with court date, ICE detention |
| VERY HIGH | Asylum approaching 1-year deadline |
| HIGH | Visa expiring within 30 days, DACA renewal |
| MODERATE | H-1B cap season, aging-out child |

## 4. AFTER-HOURS CALL SCENARIOS (Ranked by Frequency + Urgency)

1. **Criminal Arrest** — EXTREME urgency, peaks weekend nights
2. **Car Accident / PI** — HIGH urgency, highest overall frequency
3. **Domestic / Family Crisis** — HIGH urgency, evenings/weekends
4. **Immigration Emergency** — HIGH to EXTREME, geography-dependent
5. **Existing Client Check-in** — LOW urgency, very high frequency
6. **Landlord-Tenant / Eviction** — MODERATE urgency, end of month
7. **General Legal Question / Shopping** — LOW urgency, but these callers go to whoever answers first

## 5. WARM TRANSFER vs MESSAGE TAKING

### WARM TRANSFER When:
- Imminent physical danger (DV, currently unsafe)
- Currently detained/in custody
- Court appearance within 24-48 hours
- Active emergency (at accident scene, in ER, ICE at door)
- High-value case with urgency (catastrophic injury, TBI, death)
- SOL expiring within days

### MESSAGE TAKING When:
- No immediate time pressure
- Information gathering / "do I have a case?"
- Existing client status check
- Attorney shopping
- Outside business hours, non-emergency
- Case type firm doesn't handle
- Clearly disqualified

### Transfer Protocol
1. Gather name, phone, brief summary BEFORE attempting transfer
2. Announce caller to attorney with context
3. If attorney doesn't answer, take detailed message, set callback expectation
4. Mark as URGENT with time-sensitivity indicator

## 6. CONFLICT OF INTEREST SCREENING

### Information to Collect
1. Caller's full legal name (maiden name, aliases, spelling variations)
2. Date of birth
3. Opposing party's full name
4. Opposing party's insurance company (PI cases)
5. Other involved parties
6. General subject matter
7. Location/jurisdiction

### AI Agent CAN:
- Collect names of all parties during intake
- Pass names to conflict check system via API
- Report binary result: "No conflicts found" or "A potential conflict identified — attorney will review"

### AI Agent CANNOT:
- Determine whether a conflict is waivable
- Obtain informed consent for conflict waiver
- Make final accept/decline decision
- Disclose WHY there might be a conflict

---

## 7. ETHICAL GUARDRAILS

### AI CAN Say (Legal Information):
- Collect factual information
- Explain firm's practice areas and process
- Explain general next steps
- Provide publicly available legal information (general SOL timelines)
- Schedule consultations
- Explain fee structure in general terms ("contingency basis")
- Ask screening/qualification questions

### AI CANNOT Say (Legal Advice — UPL Risk):
- "You definitely have a strong case"
- "You should file a police report"
- "You'll probably get $X"
- Interpret how law applies to specific facts
- Recommend legal strategy
- Advise on settlement acceptance

### Required Opening Disclaimer
> "Thank you for calling [Firm Name]. My name is [AI Name], and I'm an AI assistant for the firm. I want to let you know upfront that I'm not an attorney and I cannot provide legal advice. What I can do is gather some information about your situation so one of our attorneys can review it and get back to you as quickly as possible. This call does not create an attorney-client relationship. Is that okay with you?"
